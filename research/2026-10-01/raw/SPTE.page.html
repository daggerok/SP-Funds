Why SPTE ETF?

Overview of SP Funds S&P Global Technology ETF (SPTE)

SPTE is an exchange-traded fund designed to track the performance of the S&P Global 1200 Shariah Information Technology (Sector) Capped index. This index includes companies from the S&P Global 1200 Shariah that belong to the Information Technology sector as classified by GICS®. The ETF aims to provide investors with exposure to global technology companies while adhering to Islamic law principles.

Investment Strategy and Composition

The SPTE focuses on large and mid-cap technology stocks from around the world, with a specific emphasis on companies pioneering in areas like e-commerce, cloud computing, and healthcare technology. As of March 31, 2026, the index comprised 102 constituents from 14 countries, with a weighted average market capitalization of approximately $1.55 trillion USD, providing a diversified portfolio aimed at capturing growth in the evolving global economy.

Shariah Compliance and Ethical Investing

The SPTE is structured in accordance with AAIOFII principles, ensuring that the investments are compliant with Islamic law. This involves excluding non-Islamic businesses and companies with high leverage. The fund undergoes rigorous screening to maintain ethical investing standards, offering a unique risk profile that aligns with Shariah guidelines.

Growth Potential and Unique Risk Profile

By investing in cutting-edge technology sectors and adhering to Shariah compliance, SPTE offers a distinctive growth opportunity for investors. The fund is positioned to benefit from the global economy’s transition towards a post-interest cycle recovery, leveraging advancements in technology to drive future returns while maintaining a unique risk profile due to its ethical investment constraints.
