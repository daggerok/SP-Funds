Why SPWO?

Overview of SP Funds S&P World ex-US ETF (SPWO)

The SP Funds S&P World ex-US ETF (SPWO) aims to replicate the performance of the S&P DM Ex-U.S. & EM 50/50 Shariah Index, excluding U.S. stocks. This ETF provides investors with exposure to Sharia-compliant investments across both emerging and developed markets globally.

Index Composition and Market Coverage

As of December 20, 2023, the index includes 500 constituent companies from 42 different countries. The average market capitalization of these companies is around US $14 billion, reflecting a diverse and broad market representation.

Growth Potential in Emerging Markets and EAFE Economies

SPWO targets companies expected to benefit from economic growth in emerging markets and regions like Europe, Australia, and the Far East (EAFE). These areas generally have lower inflation rates and higher growth prospects compared to more developed economies, providing a fertile ground for investment opportunities.

Compliance with Islamic Financial Principles

The ETF strictly adheres to Shariah law, following guidelines set by the Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions (AAOIFI). This involves excluding businesses that do not comply with Islamic principles and screening out companies with excessive leverage or income from non-compliant sources.
